The TARDIS has 3 valid forms of Energy storage
- Artron Units
- Forge Energy
- Potential Energy
In order to get Artron Energy, you must first tear a rift into the fabric of spacetime, then stabilize it, and harvest AU from it.
In order to use Forge Energy, you must hook up a valid Forge Energy system into the TARDIS Energy Port
There are 2 ways to get Potential Energy
- Kill a Weeping Angel while it is in your TARDIS
- Make the Eye Of Harmony
