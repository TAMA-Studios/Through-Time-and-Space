There are 2 methods of flying your TARDIS
- Manual Flight
- Spatial Flight
In Manual flight, you use the TARDIS console and controls to pilot it through the time vortex
In Spatial Flight, you're physically put in control of the exterior itself

Manual flight is a much more efficient, but harder to control, form of flight, whereas Spatial Flight is much more beginner-friendly, but has a much higher toll on the TARDISes subsystems and requires more fuel than ordinary flight.

Spatial flight is more efficient in planets that don't have an atmosphere, or in space.
