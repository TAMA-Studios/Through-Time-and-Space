There are currently 3 ways to obtain the TARDIS
You may either:
- Make your own fully from scratch, giving you an *Artificial* TARDIS
- Grow one, giving you a *Natural* TARDIS
- Travel to a TARDIS Graveyard and *find* a TARDIS. You may get either a Natural or an Artificial TARDIS this way.

There are 2 ways to travel to a TARDIS Graveyard
- 1: "Borrow" a friends TARDIS
- 2: Locate a rift, and either by random chance get lucky and fall into the Graveyard, or use your Sonic as a scanner to be sure it actually leads to a graveyard, and not certain doom...

To make/grow a TARDIS, see the pages on making/growing TARDISes.
