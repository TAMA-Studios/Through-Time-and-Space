Creating a TARDIS is a multi-stage process. First, the exterior shell must be assembled, then powered to create its interior dimension. Once the dimension exists, the player must establish a safe starting platform and activate the TARDIS's life-support and ARS systems.

Creating the TARDIS from scratch requires a few things
- 1x Empty Artificial Shell
- 1x Dimensional Dam Circuits
- 4x Structural Beams
- 4x Plasmic Shell Plating
- 1k Forge Energy
- 1x Dimensional Dams Circuit
- 1x TARDIS Interior Door
- 1x Oxygenator
- 1x Self Contained ARS Unit

# Assembling the shell
First, place down the Empty Artificial Shell <br />
Right-click the Empty Artificial Shell four times with Structural Beams. Each interaction installs one beam.
<br />
Once the beams have been added, add the Dimensional Dam Circuits
<br />
Finally, add all 4 Plasmic Shell Platings.
<br />
Once the shell is fully assembled, Weld it until it is finished.

# Creating the TARDIS
Now that you have your shell setup, you must power the exterior and create the interior dimension
<br />
In order to power the exterior, hook it up to a source of FE, this can be a Mekanism energy cube, another TARDIS, or something else entirely!
<br />
Once you have the exterior supplied with FE, you must wait for it to charge its internal buffer up to 1KFE
<br />
Once the exterior has reached the target 1KFE in its buffer, you may initiate the creation of the interior dimension by powering the exterior with redstone and clicking on it with the "Dimensional Dams Circuit" item.
<br />
Wait.
<br />
After waiting a while, your TARDIS's interior dimension will have been created, this is indicated by the shell having changed appearance and its doors being openable
<br />
***DO NOT ENTER THE INTERIOR DIMENSION YET***
<br />
If you enter the interior in its current state, you will suffocate to death whilst falling into the eternal void, you should generally avoid doing this as it is not healthy.
<br />
In order to prevent this, you must first do 2 things
- 1: Equip the Oxygenator, it goes in your helmet slot in your inventory (Jam it into your face)
- 2: Use the "Self Contained ARS Unit" on the Exterior, and it will generate a platform in the Interior for you to stand on and build off.
Once you have done these 2 steps, you may enter your TARDIS
