# Obtaining a TARDIS Coral
There are 2 ways to obtain the TARDIS Coral
- Use a Temporal Extractor on an already existing TARDIS
- Pull it out from a rift

# Growing a TARDIS Coral
There are 2 ways to grow the TARDIS Coral
- Place it down and wait a long time
- Craft and place a Vortex Cannon, and fire it from the Vortex Cannon (Right click the cannon with the coral in-hand to insert it, and click the cannon again to fire it)

If you decide to place it down and wait, be prepared to wait for a while as it grows!
If you decide to go with the Vortex Cannon, it will get shot into the Time Vortex and then come out as a fully grown TARDIS in much less time - At the cost of having to craft the cannon, of course.
